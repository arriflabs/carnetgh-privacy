Privacy policy

Carnet GH app · Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: your data stays on your phone. The publisher has no server, no user accounts and no advertising, and receives none of your data.

1. Who are we?

The Carnet GH app is published by ArrifLabs. Contact: arriflabs@gmail.com.

2. What data is recorded?

Only the data you enter. The app keeps one log per person followed (at most 8 active logs, for example those of a parent and their children). For each log: first name, type of log (adult, teenager or child) and colour, injection rhythm and time, recorded injections (date, time, site, dose entered, remark), missed days and the reasons given, height and weight measurements, information about cartridges and supplies, and the log's settings. In addition, there are the notes entered for the PDF summary and the app settings. This is health data. An archived log keeps its data.

3. Where is it stored?

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage. The publisher has no server and no user accounts: it does not receive, view or keep any of this data.

4. Sharing and transfers

The app sends no data to the publisher or to any third party. It contains no advertising, no audience measurement tool and no analytics service.

Two features that you start yourself create a file that you send to the app of your choice (email, cloud, printing…): the backup, which contains all logs, archived ones included, and the PDF summary for the doctor, which covers a single log. You can protect them with a password, which encrypts them. Without a password, they are not encrypted, and the name of the PDF file contains the first name when the phone has several logs. Keep them somewhere private and only share them with people you trust.

5. Automatic phone backup

To protect your data, the app is excluded from Android's automatic backup to your Google account: your data only leaves your phone through the files you create yourself. To change phones, use the backup.

6. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. They do not go through any server. With several logs, a notification may show the first name of the person concerned, including on the lock screen; with "Discreet notifications", it shows a number instead.

7. Children and teenagers

The app can follow several people on the same phone, including children and teenagers. For a minor, it is their parent or legal guardian who creates their log, enters or has their data entered, and decides on the backups and summaries shared, under their responsibility. No data is sent to the publisher.

8. Security

Your data is encrypted on the phone, with a key kept by Android's security system that never leaves the device. The app has no code of its own: its protection is your phone's screen lock (PIN, pattern, fingerprint), which we advise you to turn on. Anyone who can unlock the phone can open the app and view all logs. With the "Yes, protect management" choice (Settings › Privacy), settings, supplies, backups, PDF summaries and your own log also require unlocking the phone; viewing a child's log and recording today's injection remain possible without it.

9. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete it at any time: by deleting entries or a whole log in the app (Settings › Logs), by clearing the app's data in the Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. An archived log is no longer shown or reminded, but keeps its data until it is deleted.

10. Your rights

As the publisher holds none of your data, you view, correct, export and delete it directly in the app. For a minor, these rights are exercised by their parent or legal guardian, directly in the app. For any question: arriflabs@gmail.com. You can also contact the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr), or the data protection authority of your country.

11. Changes

This policy may change. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.

Disclaimer. This app is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. It does not calculate or recommend any dose: the doses you enter come from your prescription. It does not replace advice from a healthcare professional. In an emergency or if you are worried about your health, call your local emergency number (112 in Europe, or 15 for the SAMU in France).
