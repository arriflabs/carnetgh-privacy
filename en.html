Privacy policy

Carnet GH app · Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: your data stays on your phone. The publisher has no server, no user accounts and no advertising, and receives none of your data.

1. Who are we?

The Carnet GH app is published by ArrifLabs. Contact: arriflabs@gmail.com.

2. What data is recorded?

Only the data you enter: first name (optional), type of user, injection rhythm and time, recorded injections (date, time, site, dose entered, remark), missed days and the reasons given, height and weight measurements, information about the pen and supplies, notes entered for the PDF summary, and app settings. This is health data.

3. Where is it stored?

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage. The publisher has no server and no user accounts: it does not receive, view or keep any of this data.

4. Sharing and transfers

The app sends no data to the publisher or to any third party. It contains no advertising, no audience measurement tool and no analytics service.

Two features that you start yourself create a file that you send to the app of your choice (email, cloud, printing…): the log backup and the PDF summary for the doctor. These files are not encrypted: keep them somewhere private and only share them with people you trust.

5. Automatic phone backup

Depending on your device settings, Android may back up app data to your Google account. This backup is managed by Google and is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

6. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. They do not go through any server.

7. Children and teenagers

The app may be used for or by a child or a teenager, under the responsibility of a parent or legal guardian for minors. No data is sent to the publisher.

8. Security

Your data is protected by your phone's screen lock. The app does not encrypt it itself: make sure your device is secured (PIN, fingerprint).

9. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete it at any time: by deleting entries in the app, by clearing the app's data in the Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

10. Your rights

As the publisher holds none of your data, you view, correct, export and delete it directly in the app. For any question: arriflabs@gmail.com. You can also contact the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr), or the data protection authority of your country.

11. Changes

This policy may change. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.

Disclaimer. This app is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. It does not calculate or recommend any dose: the doses you enter come from your prescription. It does not replace advice from a healthcare professional. In an emergency or if you are worried about your health, call your local emergency number (112 in Europe, or 15 for the SAMU in France).
